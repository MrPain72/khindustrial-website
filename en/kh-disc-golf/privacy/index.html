Privacy

KH Disc Golf – Family & Friends

Privacy notice · version 0.9.1 · updated 2 November 2026

In brief: Gameplay data is primarily stored on the user's device. Raw GPS fixes, throw coordinates and travelled routes are not stored. To retrieve a weather snapshot, the current location coordinates returned by Android are sent directly to Open-Meteo without app-side rounding. Card-gift QR codes are read on the device with Google ML Kit; the image and QR-code content are not sent to Google, but ML Kit sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics to Google. Feedback and sharing transfer data to an external app only after a user-initiated and approved action.

1. Controller / publisher

KH INDUSTRIAL
Email: kimmo@khindustrial.com

2. Data stored on the device

The app may store locally in SQLite, settings and app-managed files:

  • local player profiles and names
  • rounds, games, practices, results, statistics, records and rewards
  • accepted throw distances, bearings, timestamps, disc information and available analysis snapshots
  • measurement sessions, calculated walking totals and weather snapshots
  • custom courses, course settings, practice-location names and personal ratings
  • Disc Bag information, notes and user-selected images
  • user-selected course maps
  • local card-collection data
  • settings and user-created ZIP backups.

The app requires no account or sign-in. KH INDUSTRIAL does not automatically receive this local data. The app does not use an advertising SDK, user profiling, Firebase Analytics or a separate crash-reporting service. Technical data collection by Google ML Kit, which is used for QR-code reading, is described in section 8.

3. GPS throw measurement

The user starts GPS measurement. The accepted start and endpoint latitude, longitude, accuracy and timestamp are processed in memory to calculate straight-line distance and bearing.

Only the accepted calculated result, time, selected disc and available analysis snapshot are stored. Raw fixes, their accuracy and routes are neither stored nor sent to KH INDUSTRIAL, Open-Meteo or Google ML Kit. A cancelled or rejected measurement creates no throw record.

4. Walking-distance tracking and location service

Walking-distance tracking is optional and off by default. During a user-started active round, Distance Practice or Distance Game session, location may be used by a foreground service so calculation can continue while the screen is off or the app is temporarily not visible.

GPS points are processed in memory and only the calculated total is stored locally. Raw points, routes and location history are neither stored nor sent to an external service. Tracking is stopped when the activity is completed or abandoned or when tracking is disabled. The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. Android or the device manufacturer may still interrupt the service, for example because of process termination or power management.

5. Weather snapshots and Open-Meteo

When location permission is granted and weather context is needed, the app obtains the current latitude and longitude coordinates returned by Android and sends them over HTTPS to api.open-meteo.com. The coordinates may be precise or approximate depending on the Android location permission granted by the user and the location available to the device. The app does not round or otherwise reduce the precision of the coordinates before transmission. The request asks for current temperature, weather code, wind speed, gusts and wind direction. Coordinates are not sent to a KH INDUSTRIAL server.

The returned weather values, observation time and provider name may be stored locally. The coordinates used for the request are not stored in app history. Open-Meteo states that its Free API may log IP addresses for technical and abuse-prevention purposes and that troubleshooting web-server logs may contain request URLs with geographical coordinates. It states that individual logs are deleted after 90 days and are not shared with third parties.

Provider: OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland. Open-Meteo terms and privacy.

The app uses the Open-Meteo Free API. Its terms restrict the Free API to non-commercial use, impose usage limits and require attribution under CC BY 4.0. The app displays “Weather data: Open-Meteo”. If the app's use becomes commercial, the API licence must be reassessed before distribution.

6. Feedback by email

Feedback opens the device email app with a prepared message to kimmo@khindustrial.com. It may contain user-entered text, app version and build, device manufacturer and model, Android version, possible round name, hole number and player count, and up to five user-selected images that are resized locally.

KH Disc Golf does not send email in the background. The user reviews the recipient and content and approves sending in the email app. The user's email provider, transmission services and KH INDUSTRIAL's email provider then process the message under their own terms. Received feedback remains in the recipient mailbox until deleted. The user may ask KH INDUSTRIAL to delete feedback under its control by sending a request to kimmo@khindustrial.com.

7. Sharing scorecards and card gifts

When the user chooses to share, the app creates a local PNG image and opens Android sharing. The image is transferred only to the app or service selected by the user, whose privacy terms govern further processing. The app does not share automatically or select a recipient.

8. QR-code reading and Google ML Kit

When a user chooses to import a card gift from an image, the user selects the image with the Android system picker and the app reads its QR code with the Google ML Kit barcode scanner. The QR image and decoded content are processed on the device. Under Google's ML Kit terms, the image, QR-code content and recognition result are not sent to Google servers.

Android automatically starts the ML Kit initializer included in the app when the app process starts, before any possible QR import. Google states that ML Kit collects technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics, including device manufacturer, model, OS version and build, available ML hardware accelerators, app package name and version, a per-installation identifier, performance and latency metrics, API configuration, input and output size, feature version, event types and error codes. ML Kit may also contact Google servers to receive fixes, models or hardware-accelerator compatibility information.

Google states that this technical data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not shared with third parties. It is used for ML Kit diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, improvement and abuse detection. This is not advertising or user-profiling analytics operated by KH INDUSTRIAL. Further information: ML Kit data disclosure and ML Kit terms and privacy.

9. Images, files and backups

Course maps, disc images, feedback images, card-gift images containing QR codes and backups are selected with Android system pickers. The app does not receive general access to the photo library and does not request camera or microphone permission. Course and disc images are copied to local app storage. Temporary optimized feedback copies are stored in cache; older feedback copies are removed when new feedback is prepared.

A user-created ZIP backup may contain the SQLite database, settings, course maps and disc images. The app does not send backups to KH INDUSTRIAL. The user controls the destination and any later sharing.

10. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until deleted through app functions or by removing the app and its data. Separately saved backups and files shared to external apps must be deleted from those destinations. Android cloud backup and automatic device-to-device app-data transfer are disabled by the app's backup rules.

The user may ask KH INDUSTRIAL to delete feedback under its control by sending an email to kimmo@khindustrial.com. KH INDUSTRIAL cannot directly delete or promise deletion of data held by Open-Meteo, Google, the user's email provider or a sharing destination selected by the user. Those services apply their own retention and deletion procedures. Open-Meteo's stated log retention is described in section 5 and Google ML Kit processing in section 8.

11. Network and permissions

Release 0.9.1 uses ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION for location features. Internet access is used for Open-Meteo weather requests, Google ML Kit technical connections, and user-opened Google Play and privacy links. Email and sharing transfers take place through the external app chosen by the user.

The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION, broad storage, camera, microphone or Advertising ID permission.

12. Children and accounts

The app requires no account, email address or sign-in. Players may be added locally by name. The Google Play target audience and age rating are configured separately in Play Console.

13. Changes and contact

This policy is updated when the app's data handling changes.

Privacy questions, questions about local data, and requests concerning feedback sent to KH INDUSTRIAL: kimmo@khindustrial.com.
