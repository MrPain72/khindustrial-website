KH Disc Golf – Family & Friends

Built for rounds with family and friends, and for focused practice. One phone is enough for the group, and there are no mandatory accounts or sign-ins.

Record scores quickly, share a finished scorecard and follow each player's progress. The core playing and scoring experience works offline, with data stored locally on the device.

KH Disc Golf 0.9.1 – A Whole New Way to Play & Practice!

Throw. Measure. Challenge your friends. Improve. The major update introduced in version 0.9.0 created a completely new way to practise. Release 36 (0.9.1), including an Android startup fix, brought these features to the selected Google Play closed Alpha test group; the latest Play update date is 15 October 2026.

GPS throw distance

Measure from an individual or shared start point

Measure each throw from its own start point or use one shared start for the entire measurement round. GPS results are approximate estimates that you approve before saving.

Multi-round practice

Build your own throwing session

Choose 1–10 discs and 1–10 measurement rounds. Change discs and continue with the same or a new selection for the next round.

Records and analysis

See what flies farthest – and how

Follow each disc's overall, backhand and forehand records. Throw analysis uses available disc, direction, weather and wind data.

Family & Friends

Challenge your friends on one phone

Keep friend discs in their own section, run a multiplayer distance-throwing competition and share one scorecard for the whole group.

History and sharing

Review your throws and follow progress

Practice history brings together sessions, measurement rounds, throws and analyses. Preview and share a completed practice result.

Clearer course rounds

Get a round started more smoothly

A more compact scoring view and redesigned round setup and player selection keep the focus on playing.

In-app guide

Guidance in Finnish and English

The updated guide explains rounds, GPS measurement, practice, records and group games step by step.

Rounds and scorecards

Play together and follow your progress

One phone for the group

Add several players, record hole-by-hole scores and keep an individual history for each player.

Flexible rounds

Start on any hole, temporarily exclude holes and resume an unfinished round later. Reuse a previous round's settings.

History, records and sharing

Review round history and personal course and hole records. Share a completed scorecard as an image.

My Profile

Your playing history at a glance

The primary player's local profile brings together rounds, holes played, throws, courses, personal records and Card Deck statistics. Change the name without losing the player's history.

Courses and maps

Ready-made courses and your own layouts

Course library

Browse included courses with holes, pars, distances and course maps. Search by location and mark favourites.

Your own courses

Create a course, define its holes and update the details later.

Maps and ratings

Add your own map image and save a personal rating from one to five stars.

Disc Bag

Your discs, photos and flight estimates

Save a disc's name, manufacturer, plastic, colour, weight, stability, wear, purchase details, rating, preference, notes and photos. Search, filter and mark favourites.

Flight numbers

Speed · Glide · Turn · Fade

Keep the four standard flight numbers together in a clear view.

Photos

Inspect the details

Open a disc photo full screen and zoom in to check details such as flight numbers.

Offline

Flight estimate and simulator

A local visual estimate illustrates the disc's flight characteristics without an online service.

Card Deck

Collect by playing and give cards to friends

501 collectible cards

Follow the collection and filter by rarity or acquisition method. Every card has a permanent KHDG identifier.

PLAYED and GIFTED

A completed round can award a PLAYED card. A card received by QR image is saved as GIFTED while the giver keeps their own card.

Finnish and English

Card text and explanations follow the app language. The glossary also helps explain disc golf terms and flight numbers.

Practice and mini-games

More ways to enjoy disc golf

Flick

An eight-hole offline mini-game for one or two players, with individual scores and a shared Record Board · TOP 6.

Discs into the cage

A relaxed garden game and accuracy exercise to play alone or with a group.

Guides

A glossary and beginner's rules guide explain common situations and Speed, Glide, Turn and Fade.

Simple and private

Start playing without a required account

Core play and scorekeeping work without a network connection. Player, course, round, score, Card Deck and Disc Bag data are stored locally on the device.

Weather snapshots use an internet connection to Open-Meteo. The user controls the app's ZIP backups.
